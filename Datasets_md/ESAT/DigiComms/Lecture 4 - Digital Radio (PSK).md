- is similar to conventional phase modulation except hat with PSK the input signal is a binary digital signal, and a limited number of output phases are possible.

## QUATERNARY PSK

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

- Quaternary phase shift keying (QPSK), or quadrature PSK as it is sometimes called, is another form of angle-modulated, constant-amplitude digital modulation.
- QPSK  is  an  M-ary  encoding  scheme  where  N  =  2  and  M  =  4 (hence, the name 'quaternary' meaning ' 4 ')
- With two bits, there are four possible conditions: 00, 01, 10, and 11.
- In  the  modulator,  each  dibit  code  generates  one  of  the  four possible output phases (+45 ° , +135 ° , -45 ° , and -135 ° ).
