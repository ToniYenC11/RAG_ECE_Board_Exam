## THYRISTORS

It is a solid-state semiconductor device with four layers of alternating P-type &amp; N-type semiconductor. It acts as a bi-stable switch, that will conduct when the gate terminal receives a current trigger

## Silicon-Controlled Rectifiers (SCR)

SCR is a trade name of General Electric for their type of Thyristor developed in 1957; i.e. "Pampers" for Diapers; Colgate" for toothpaste; etc.
